Privacy Policy — Receipt Keeper

Effective date: October 7, 2026
Applies to: Receipt Keeper (Android), by CreatorSprout
Contact: hello@creatorsprout.com

The short version

Receipt Keeper stores everything on your device. We collect no receipts, no photos, no personal data, no analytics. Your financial documents never leave your phone — there is nothing to sell, share, or leak, because we never receive it.

What the app does on your device

  • Scans receipts with the camera only when you tap to scan, and extracts store, date, total, tax, and line items using on-device OCR (Google ML Kit) — images are processed locally, never uploaded.
  • You may also pick receipt photos through the Android system picker. The app does not request photo/media library permissions.
  • All receipts, stores, items, and preferences are stored in a local database only on your device.
  • Month-end PDF reports are generated on your phone; emailing a report opens your own email app — we never see the recipient or the content.
  • Local notifications remind you when the monthly report is ready; they are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device.
  • The app requests no internet permission and includes no analytics or advertising SDKs.

What we collect

Nothing. We have no servers, no accounts, and no way to access your data.

Camera permission

Android grants the app camera access solely so you can photograph receipts. The camera is never activated except when you choose to scan, and captured images are used only for on-device text recognition and stored in the app's private storage.

Receipt Keeper Pro (in-app purchases)

Receipt Keeper is free: 20 receipt scans per calendar month. Receipt Keeper Pro unlocks unlimited scans:

  • $4.99/month
  • $29.99/year
  • $14.99 one-time — lifetime access, no renewal

All purchases are processed by Google Play under the Google Play Terms of Service — we never see your card information.

Children

Receipt Keeper is not directed at children under 13, and we knowingly collect no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
